This 112-day roadmap is a foundation, not a promise that become an expert in AI within 112 days.
Research-level ability requires further practice, deeper mathematics, substantial projects and real engineering datasets.

immediate target is simple: finish Python fundamentals first. Don't rush into Machine Learning before you're comfortable with Python.

# **Python → Machine Learning → AI for Civil Engineering**

## **Complete Day-by-Day Journey Plan**



Personal learning roadmap

**Study structure:**

- Daily lesson: 15 minutes.
- Coding practice: 15–30 minutes when possible.
- Project/revision days: spend extra time when available.
- Language: English lessons, with C-to-Python comparisons.
- IDE: Google Colab initially, then VS Code.
